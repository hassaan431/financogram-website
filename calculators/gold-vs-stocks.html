What "actual" means here: the gold line uses annual average 24K gold price per tola; the stocks line uses KSE-100 year-end close — both from 2005 to 2025. Editing a return % switches that line from real history to a smooth custom projection (dashed), useful for what-if scenarios.

Dividends: the KSE-100 line is a price index — it excludes dividends. Pakistani equities have historically paid meaningful dividend yields on top of this, so real stock returns for a dividend-reinvesting investor would run higher than shown.

Gold data: unlike KSE-100 (PSX official data), there's no single authoritative historical archive for Pakistani gold prices. These are directional/consensus averages.

Sources: KSE-100 — Pakistan Stock Exchange. Gold — market-average 24K tola rates. Inflation — Pakistan Bureau of Statistics / World Bank CPI annual averages.
