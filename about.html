Teaching What School Never Did

Our mission is to educate the youth of Pakistan about personal finance, investing, and long-term wealth building.

The Story Behind Financogram

Money dictates almost every aspect of our lives, yet the vast majority of us graduate from school without a single lesson on how to manage it. We are taught complex calculus, but we aren't taught how to budget, save, or invest.

Financogram was built to fix that gap. We believe that financial literacy shouldn't be a privilege reserved for a few. It should be accessible, easy to understand, and highly actionable. By breaking down complex financial concepts into simple, jargon-free lessons, we aim to empower the youth of Pakistan to take control of their financial future.

Meet the Founder

While I've spent years dealing with complex data and systems, my true passion lies in something much simpler: understanding money. I absolutely love to learn and educate others about personal finance.

I started Financogram because I realized that the foundational rules of saving, investing, and growing wealth aren't taught in school, leaving millions of smart young people to figure it out the hard way. My goal is to change that.
