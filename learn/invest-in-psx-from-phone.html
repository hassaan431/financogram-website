❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Are my shares safe? What happens if Nafa goes down?

Your money and shares do not sit with Nafa as a tech startup. Nafa is an intuitive digital frontend powered by Chase Securities, an SECP-licensed broker and PSX TREC holder. More importantly, all your purchased shares are registered directly in your own name and CNIC with the Central Depository Company of Pakistan (CDC). Even if the app were unavailable, you retain legal ownership of your shares at the central custodian level.

What is the Central Gateway Portal (CGP)?

The Central Gateway Portal (CGP) is operated by the National Clearing Company of Pakistan Limited (NCCPL). It acts as a centralized national digital repository for biometric KYC records. Once you verify your CNIC and identity on CGP, you can effortlessly link multiple brokers without resubmitting physical identity paperwork.

Do I need a large amount of money to start?

No! There is no minimum deposit requirement. You can start investing with as little as a few hundred rupees by purchasing odd-lots or single shares of solid dividend-paying blue-chip Pakistani companies.

How can I fund my account?

You can fund your trading account via 1Link 1Bill or online bank transfer (IBFT) from any Pakistani bank account registered under your own CNIC.

How do I transfer shares from my old broker to Nafa?

First, complete your Nafa account opening so your new Chase Securities sub-account number is generated. Then, contact your existing broker and request an intra-account or inter-broker CDC transfer form. You will provide your Chase Securities account details, and CDC will transfer your share certificates electronically without requiring you to sell and trigger capital gains tax (CGT).
