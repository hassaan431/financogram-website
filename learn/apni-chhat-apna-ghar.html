Apni Chhat Apna Ghar: Punjab’s Interest-Free House Loan, Explained

Up to Rs 15 lakh, strictly 0% markup, for citizens who already own a plot. Who qualifies, what you repay, and how to apply on the official portal.

By Financogram  ·  Updated 29 October 2026  ·  6 min read

The Short Version

What it is
An interest-free loan to build a house on a plot you already own. Not a free house, and it doesn’t buy land.
Maximum loan
Rs 15 lakh (Rs 1,500,000), released in tranches as construction progresses.
Repayment
No markup, over 9 years. Exactly Rs 13,889 a month on the full Rs 15 lakh.
Plot size
Up to 5 marla (city or town), up to 10 marla (village).
Official Portal
acag.punjab.gov.pk (the only authentic site). Official Helpline: 0800-09100.

What it is

Many families in Pakistan own a small plot or family land but simply cannot afford the soaring upfront cost of bricks, cement, steel, and labor to construct a house. Commercial bank mortgages typically carry 12% to 22% annual markup, turning a Rs 15 lakh loan into over Rs 25 to 30 lakh in total repayments over a decade.

Under the Apni Chhat Apna Ghar (ACAG) initiative, the Government of Punjab provides an interest-free loan where you repay strictly the principal amount you borrowed—with zero bank markup and zero hidden processing charges. The programme is managed by the Punjab Housing and Town Planning Agency (PHATA), while microfinance and banking partners—including the Bank of Punjab, Akhuwat, NRSP, and RCDP—handle site verifications and disbursements.

Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz announced the scheme in August 2024, and the first disbursement cheques were handed over on 2 October 2024. By late September 2026, official dashboard metrics indicated that over 212,531 loans worth roughly Rs 274 billion had been disbursed across Punjab—averaging approximately Rs 12.9 lakh per loan—with more than 119,800 houses already completed. On 19 October 2026, the Punjab Cabinet approved a fresh allocation of Rs 60 billion to fund an additional 40,000 loans.

Who Can Apply

  • Punjab Domicile: You must be a permanent resident of Punjab (verified via your CNIC).
  • Plot Ownership: You must be the sole owner of a plot up to 5 marla in urban areas or up to 10 marla in rural areas. If the plot was inherited, the official transfer (inteqal/fard) must already be completed in your name.
  • Clean Credit Record: You must have no criminal convictions and no unpaid financial defaults with microfinance institutions or commercial banks.
  • Registered SIM: The mobile phone SIM card used during portal registration must be registered in your own CNIC name.
Confirm these two items with PHATA helpline before applying

Documentation across partner agencies shows slight discrepancies regarding the official age brackets (the ACAG portal guide lists ages 21 to 60, whereas one microfinance partner listed ages 18 to 45). Similarly, whether a poverty PMT (Proxy Means Test) score of 60 or below applies strictly to roof repair loans or also to new house builds can be verified via the official helpline at 0800-09100.

What You Will Repay

Because the loan is 100% interest-free, the repayment mathematics is straightforward and predictable:

Monthly Installment = Total Loan Amount ÷ 108 Months (9 Years)

Rs 10 lakh (Rs 1,000,000) Rs 9,259 / mo Rs 1,000,000
Rs 15 lakh (Rs 1,500,000) Rs 13,889 / mo Rs 1,500,000
Simple linear division over 108 monthly cycles. Maximum scheme cap is Rs 15 lakh.
Note on 9-Year vs. 7-Year Tenor

At scheme inception, the Chief Minister announced a Rs 14,000 monthly cap over 9 years (108 months), which corresponds to the math shown above. A later portal briefing note mentioned a 7-year option (which would yield installments of approximately Rs 17,860/month). Always review the final sanction letter issued by PHATA/BOP before signing.

What “Interest-Free” Actually Saves You

To grasp the financial advantage of zero markup, compare this with borrowing Rs 15 lakh from a commercial bank over 9 years at a standard 12% annual interest rate. A commercial loan would require approximately Rs 22,776 each month, totaling Rs 24.6 lakh over the loan life. That means the Punjab Government's subsidy saves you roughly Rs 9.6 lakh in markup alone.

Can your household afford it? In personal finance, the standard debt-to-income benchmark recommends that total housing debt installments should not exceed one-third (33%) of your steady monthly household income. At Rs 13,889 per month, a household earning Rs 42,000 or above comfortably satisfies this prudent ratio.

Will Rs 15 Lakh Be Enough to Finish the House?

In most urban and semi-urban parts of Punjab, constructing a complete 5-marla grey structure with basic electrical, plumbing, and plaster typically ranges between Rs 20 lakh and Rs 28 lakh, depending on materials and site conditions. Therefore, the Rs 15 lakh loan acts as a foundational catalyst rather than a total construction package.

Your Own Cash Needed = Total Contractor Estimate − Approved Loan Amount

For example, if your vetted contractor quotes Rs 22 lakh for construction, the ACAG loan provides Rs 15 lakh, leaving a funding balance of Rs 7 lakh for your family to contribute. Furthermore, because funds arrive in staged tranches linked to construction milestones (foundation, lintel, and roof), you should maintain sufficient cash flow to purchase materials while waiting for verification officers to inspect each stage.

Step-by-Step: How to Apply Online

  1. Visit the Official Portal: Open your web browser and go to acag.punjab.gov.pk. Select “Apply for Construction of New House”.
  2. Create Your Account: Register with your full legal name (matching your CNIC), CNIC number, district/tehsil, and mobile number. Confirm your registration using the SMS OTP code sent to your phone.
  3. Complete Application Form: Log in using your credentials, fill out the property location details (khasra/khewat numbers or town address), and upload high-resolution scans of your original CNIC and certified land record (Fard / Registered Sale Deed).
  4. Physical Plot Verification: Once initial automated screening is completed, an inspection officer from a designated partner (Bank of Punjab, Akhuwat, NRSP, or RCDP) will visit your plot to confirm boundary demarcations and legal possession.
  5. Tranche Releases: Upon approval, funds are transferred in installments directly to your verified account. After finishing a stage (e.g. foundation), use the portal’s “Request Next Tranche” button to initiate milestone verification.

Need Assistance? Call the toll-free Government Helpline at 0800-09100, or contact PHATA headquarters at 042-99213419. If you do not have digital access, paper application facilitation desks are operational at designated Deputy Commissioner (DC), Assistant Commissioner (AC), and PHATA district offices across Punjab.

Beware of Scams & Unofficial Portals
  • The official portal is hosted strictly on acag.punjab.gov.pk. Beware of copycat websites ending in .com, .xyz, or .club pretending to be government registration portals.
  • Registration is 100% free: The Punjab government does not charge any registration, token, or file processing fees. Never hand cash to private middlemen or agents claiming to guarantee loan selection.
  • Do not forward your CNIC or personal bank details to viral SMS codes (such as “8770”) circulating on WhatsApp; no such short code is endorsed by PHATA.

Alternative Schemes to Consider

If you do not meet the criteria for the ACAG 15 lakh plot construction loan, consider these related initiatives:

  • No Land Owned: The Apni Zameen Apna Ghar scheme distributes free 3-marla residential plots to underprivileged families via transparent computerized balloting in select districts.
  • Existing Home Needing Repair / Additional Floor: The Apni Chhat, Mehfooz Chhat (ACMC) programme offers interest-free loans of Rs 5 lakh (roof repair/room addition) or Rs 10 lakh (second storey expansion) with installments as low as Rs 4,630/month.
  • Purchasing Ready Built Houses via Banks: If you seek commercial financing or higher loan limits, check out the national housing schemes. You can simulate your payments using our dedicated calculator below.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Apni Chhat Apna Ghar loan genuinely interest-free?

Yes. The Government of Punjab pays all financial service fees, management costs, and operational taxes directly to partner financial institutions. Beneficiaries repay strictly 100% of the principal amount borrowed, with zero markup or hidden bank charges.

Can I repay the loan before the 9-year period ends?

Secondary operational guidelines report a 20% rebate on outstanding principal if settled early within years 1 to 3, and a 10% rebate if settled within years 4 to 6. Always verify the early retirement terms in your official agreement with your loan partner.

What occurs if an applicant misses a monthly installment?

While interest-free schemes do not accrue compound interest, default terms are legally binding under your promissory agreement and mortgage lien on the property deed. Ensure you review the grace period rules with your lending partner.

How long does approval and physical verification take?

Verification durations vary by district and backlog. Applications undergo automated NADRA/land registry validation within 7 to 14 days, followed by on-site plot verification by field officers before cheque or tranche release.
