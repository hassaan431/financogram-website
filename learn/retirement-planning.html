How to Plan Your Retirement (Without Losing Your Mind)

By Financogram  ·  4 min read

When you're young, retirement feels like a concept reserved for your grandparents. Why worry about what happens 30 years from now when you have rent, groceries, and weekend plans to pay for today?

Here's the harsh truth: the government won't pay for your lifestyle, and relying entirely on a pension (if you even have one) is risky. Retirement planning isn't about being old; it's about buying your future freedom.

1. Start With the Magic of Compounding

The single greatest asset you have in your 20s and 30s isn't a high salary—it's time. Thanks to compound interest, the money you invest today will grow exponentially.

If you invest PKR 10,000 every month for 30 years at a 15% annual return (historical PSX average), you won't just double your money. You'll turn your total contribution of PKR 3.6 million into over PKR 7 Crore. The catch? You have to start now.

2. Estimate Your "Magic Number"

How much do you actually need to retire? A popular rule of thumb is the Rule of 25 (which relates to the 4% withdrawal rule). To figure out your target retirement corpus:

  • Estimate your desired annual living expenses in retirement.
  • Multiply that number by 25.

If you need PKR 24 Lakh a year to live comfortably, your target corpus is roughly PKR 6 Crore. While inflation complicates this exact math in Pakistan, having a concrete goal gives you something to aim at rather than just blindly saving.

3. Beat Inflation with the Right Assets

In Pakistan, parking your money in a standard savings account is a guaranteed way to lose wealth. Inflation will eat your purchasing power alive.

To plan a successful retirement, your money needs to grow faster than inflation. This usually means a mix of:

  • Equities (PSX/Mutual Funds): High growth potential over 10+ years to beat inflation.
  • Real Estate: Tangible asset that historically appreciates and provides rental income.
  • Money Market / Fixed Income: Safer, highly liquid assets for short-term stability.

4. Automate and Forget

Human discipline is unreliable. The best retirement plan is one you don't have to think about. Set up a Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) that automatically deducts a percentage of your salary the day after you get paid. If you don't see the money, you won't spend it.
